# BEYOND Collection — Image Generation Prompts

This document lists the exact photographic prompts used for the BEYOND soft/pastel collection. You can copy-paste these prompts into Google Gemini, Imagen 3, Midjourney v6, or any modern image generation model to generate additional products or angles and drop them into `tools/raw/<slug>/`.

---

## Global Photographic Style Specification
Every image uses these shared style guidelines for consistent studio framing:
- **Framing & View**: 4:5 portrait aspect ratio, perfectly centered, hanging naturally on a dark walnut wooden hanger with a small silver metal hook.
- **Lighting & Backdrop**: Seamless solid neutral off-white / light grey studio wall, soft diffused softbox lighting with gentle contact shadow, zero harsh highlights.
- **Garment Quality**: Sharp, tactile fabric textures (wool, leather grain, denim twill, French terry fleece, linen slub), visible realistic stitching, no models or mannequins, clean silhouettes.
- **Angle Variations**:
  - `front`: Frontal view facing the camera.
  - `three-quarter`: Rotated 30°–45° left.
  - `side`: Rotated 90° side profile showing sleeve and side hem.
  - `back`: Rear view facing away from camera.
